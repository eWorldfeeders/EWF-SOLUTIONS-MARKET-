About eWorldfeeders Global Link

Bridging Technology, Agriculture, Energy & Human Development

eWorldfeeders Global Link is a Nigerian technology-driven enterprise focused on developing and coordinating practical solutions across technology, agriculture, renewable energy, business transformation and human development.

Our approach is built around identifying real-world problems, connecting the right technical capabilities and partnerships, and transforming ideas into useful systems, services and opportunities.

We also collaborate with eMicro Multipurpose ICT Firm (EMIF), a specialized ICT company that provides technical ICT capabilities and implementation support where required.

Living For Humanity Is Our Number One

Our Vision

To contribute to a thriving, zero-hunger world powered by precision agriculture, clean energy and universal technical literacy.

Our Mission

To develop practical technology and AgroTech solutions, build technical capacity and create collaborative frameworks that support sustainable development.

Our Approach

We coordinate people, technology, knowledge and strategic partnerships around real problems. The objective is not simply to sell technology, but to create solutions that can be useful, sustainable and scalable.

About EWF

Connecting Innovation.
Creating Value. Empowering People.

eWorldfeeders Global Link is a Nigerian technology-driven enterprise building and coordinating practical solutions across technology, agriculture, renewable energy, business transformation and human development.

Our marketplace brings together solution areas, technical capabilities and strategic partnerships so individuals, businesses, organizations and communities can move from a real-world problem toward a practical solution.

For specialized ICT implementation, EWF collaborates with eMicro Multipurpose ICT Firm (EMIF).

Living For Humanity Is Our Number One

Our Vision

To contribute to a thriving, zero-hunger world powered by precision agriculture, clean energy and universal technical literacy.

Our Mission

To develop practical technology and AgroTech solutions, build technical capacity and create collaborative frameworks that support sustainable development.

Our Approach

We coordinate people, technology, knowledge and strategic partnerships around real problems. The objective is not simply to sell technology, but to create solutions that can be useful, sustainable and scalable.
